Audio Survey

Hello and thank you for helping with this project!
Please listen to the audio clips and answer the shown questions.
Plesee do not change your answers after being shown the (mis)heard text and the original audio text.
There are two types of audio samples in this survey: "Dirty Audio" that has been intentionally distorted with volume and speed changes, noise and interruptions, and "Clean Audio" that has not.
Both may still be spoken in dialect or accents
You can answer as many questions as you want and have time, but please try to work on a similar amount of clean and dirty audio samples.
When you are finished, please click the "Go back to the Main Page" link at the bottom of this page to prepare for the next participant.
